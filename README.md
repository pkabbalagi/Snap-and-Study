# 📚 Snap & Study

## AI-Powered Study Assistant

Snap & Study is an AI-powered educational application that helps students understand study material using image analysis and conversational AI.

Students can upload images of textbooks, handwritten notes, diagrams, or study material. The application analyzes the uploaded content and provides a simple explanation, important points, key definitions, exam-focused points, and quick revision notes.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Help students understand difficult study material easily.
- Analyze uploaded study images using AI.
- Provide simple and student-friendly explanations.
- Generate important exam points and definitions.
- Support follow-up questions through conversational AI.
- Generate quick revision summaries.
- Allow students to download their study summaries.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📷 Study Image Analysis
Students can upload JPG, JPEG, or PNG images of their study material.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Explanation
The application uses Google's Gemini AI to understand the uploaded content.

### 💬 Interactive Questions
Students can ask follow-up questions about the study material.

### 📝 Exam Preparation
The system can provide:
- 2-mark answers
- 5-mark answers
- 10-mark answers
- Important points
- Definitions
- Viva questions
- Quick revision notes

### 📚 Study Summary
The application creates a concise revision summary from the AI-generated explanations.

### ⬇️ Download
Students can download the generated study summary as a text file.
